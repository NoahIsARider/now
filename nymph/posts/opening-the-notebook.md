This is the first entry. Nymph is the writing desk behind the rest of the
site: the films live in MAYDAY, the records in shkb, the books in babel, and
whatever those lists cannot hold ends up here.

Expect working notes rather than finished essays. Something I half-understand
after reading a paper, a sentence that would not leave me alone, a build log
from a small project, an argument I am still having with myself. The point is
to write while the idea is still warm.

New pieces appear at the top of the front page. The feed is linked in the
bar above if you would rather have things delivered.
